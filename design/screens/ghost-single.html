The woman on the third-floor landing

She was there every night for a week. My daughter described her before I ever did.

Witness
Dana R.
Location
Savannah, Georgia
When
March 2024
Experience
Apparition

Reported site

A rented townhouse on East Jones Street

We rented the place for eleven months, and for ten of them nothing happened at all. That is the part people never believe. It was an ordinary house with an ordinary staircase and a landing on the third floor where the previous tenants had left a hook in the wall.

The first time was a Tuesday in March. I came up at about half past one in the morning because my daughter was awake and had been for a while, and there was a woman standing at the far end of the landing with her back to the window.

I want to be careful here, because I have read enough of these accounts to know how they usually go. She was not transparent. She was not glowing. She did not move toward me or speak or do anything at all. She was a woman in a gray dress standing in a house where only three people lived, and I stood there long enough to know I was looking at someone.

My daughter said, before I had said anything: "She stands there most nights."

That is what unsettled me, and it is still what unsettles me. My daughter was six. She had not mentioned it once. When I asked her why, she said she thought the woman lived there too.

It happened five more times over the following week, always between one and two in the morning, always the same position on the landing. Then it stopped, and it never happened again in the seven weeks before we moved out.

I have no photographs. I did not try to take any. I know how that sounds, and I have made my peace with it — standing on a staircase at half past one in the morning, taking a picture was not what I thought about doing.
